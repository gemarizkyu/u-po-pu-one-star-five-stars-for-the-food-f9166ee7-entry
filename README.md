## Entry

One star: Earth sells you water that falls free from the sky and charges rent just to sleep, but one plate of smoky sate made the whole invasion worth it.

## How it works

main.py holds the sentence as a fixed constant and writes it to /output/entry.txt. No network, no model calls, no randomness, no wall clock. Before writing, it validates the sentence (UTF-8, 1-200 chars, one sentence).
